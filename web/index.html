About LifePod

Privacy

LifePod collects no data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the apps make no network requests.

The current game (players, life totals, seat rotations, tile colours, counters, floating mana, turns, and settings) is saved only on this device. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Full policy: github.com/geota/lifepod/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
