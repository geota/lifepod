LifePod keeps every player's life total in front of them. Set your phone in the middle of the table and each total faces its player's seat.

• 2 to 6 players, with seats facing every side of the table
• Tap anywhere on your half of the tile to change your total, or hold to change it fast
• Several players can tap at the same time
• Rotate any seat to match where people are sitting
• Give each player their own tile colour from nine
• Optional commander tax, colour identity and poison counters, shown only when set
• Commander damage on a map of the table, taken from life in the same tap
• Warnings as commander damage or poison nears the losing amount
• Seats stay in place when the phone turns
• Start at 20, 30, 40, or any life total you choose
• Roll a d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 or d20 with true randomness
• Totals at 0 or below turn red
• Keeps the screen awake during the game
• Saves your game if you leave the app

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no internet permission. LifePod works entirely offline and collects no data.

Built for Commander and other Magic: The Gathering formats, LifePod works for any game that tracks life or points.
