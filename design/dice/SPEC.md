# Dice roller: design spec

**Status:** applied in `web/dice.js`, `web/app.js` and `web/styles.css`. The app uses `#f49993` for life totals at 0 or below. Each die now has its own colour; see "Die colours" below.

The muted, dark palette stays as it is. This adds one die body (pale sage "bone", built from the palette's greens) and one red for life totals that hit zero. Every value below is in `dice.css` (tokens and reference CSS), `shapes.js` (geometry) and `dice.js` (drawing, randomness, animation), and all of it is drawn in `renders/`.

Files:

- `shapes.js`: geometry for the six dice. Generated by `build-shapes.mjs`.
- `dice.css`: new tokens plus reference CSS for the pill, the sheet, the die and the life-out colour. It loads after `web/styles.css`.
- `dice.js`: draws the die and the picker icon with `createElementNS`, rolls with `crypto.getRandomValues`, and plays the WAAPI animation.
- `mock-app.js`, `render.mjs`, `preview.html/.css/.js`: the mock-up and renders. `node design/dice/render.mjs` serves the repo and writes `renders/*.png`. `preview.html` needs that HTTP server too, because ES modules don't load from file://.

## 1. Entry point: a dice | setup pill at the board centre

Your working idea holds. Keep it **horizontal in board coordinates for every layout**. The pill lives inside `.app`, so it turns with the board and is always horizontal relative to the landscape board.

- Markup: wrap the existing `#setup-button` in `<div class="board-controls">` and put `<button class="dice-button" id="dice-button" aria-label="Roll dice" aria-haspopup="dialog">` before it. Focus order is dice, then setup.
- Size: two 48 × 48 targets, so 96 × 48 in all. The visible pill is 84 × 36 (inset 6px), with the same 1px `--control-border` ring, `--background` fill and `--shadow-float` as today's disc. A 1px `--border` divider runs from 14px to 34px at the centre. Each half shows `--surface-pressed` inside the pill on `:active`. Icons are 20px (`font-size: 1.25rem`) in `--text-soft`.
- What it covers: compared with today's 48px disc it adds 48 × 48. In 4p that is 24 × 24 more at each tile's inner corner. In 5p and 6p the centre falls on the horizontal seam inside the middle column, so the pill lies along that seam. In 2p and 3p it crosses the vertical seam by 42px into tiles that are half the screen wide. In none of these does it reach a − or + button (`renders/board-*.png`).
- Seat rotation: set `data-rotating` on `.board-controls`. The dice button and divider hide, and the pill collapses to today's single 36px accent Done disc (`renders/board-4p-rotating.png`). The dice dialog can't open during rotation.
- Icon: **Phosphor `dice-five`, Bold**. It reads as "dice" at 20px and looks the same at every quarter turn, so it never looks sideways on a turned board. The renders use a stand-in drawn to its geometry, so fetch the real path.

## 2. The dice sheet

A `<dialog id="dice" aria-labelledby="dice-title">` that reuses `.sheet-header`, `.close` and `.primary`. Its parts:

- **Header**: "Dice" and a close button.
- **Stage**: `<button class="dice-stage" aria-label="Roll a d20">`. Tapping the die rolls it. The stage is a `--background` well with a 1px `--border` edge and an 18px radius (`--radius-lg`). The die is 74% of its smaller side (`74cqmin`; the stage is a size container).
- **Picker**: `<div class="dice-picker" role="group" aria-label="Sides">` with six `aria-pressed` buttons (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20), in a 3 × 2 grid with a 4px gap. Each button is at least 48px tall and shows a 22px die icon, a 6px gap and the label "d20" (`--type-label`, semibold). The selected button uses the accent fill with `--ink`, like the setup choices.
- **Roll**: `.primary.dice-roll`, 48px tall, 17px text, labelled "Roll d20" (with the current die).
- **Announcer**: a `.visually-hidden` live region that says "Rolled 17 on a d20" once the die settles.

Layout:

| | Portrait (320 × 568 and up) | Landscape (568 × 320 and up) |
|---|---|---|
| Dialog width | `min(420px, 100% − 32px)` | `fit-content`, at most `100% − 32px` |
| Grid | header / stage / picker / roll | stage on the left across all rows; header, picker and roll in a right column of 212–260px |
| Stage | full width, `block-size: clamp(176px, 100dvh − 340px, 340px)` | square, `min(100dvh − 38px, 400px)` on both axes |
| Padding / gaps | 12px 16px 16px, 12px gaps | 12px padding, 16px column gap, 12px row gap; picker sits under the header, Roll at the bottom |

At 568 × 320 the stage is 282px and the right column 214px (`renders/sheet-568x320.png`). At 320 × 568 the stage is 256 × 228 (`renders/sheet-320x568.png`). Tablets get a 400px stage in landscape and a full-width 340px stage in portrait (`sheet-1024x768.png`, `sheet-768x1024.png`). Because `.dice-stage` has size containment, the landscape rule must set `inline-size` explicitly.

Behaviour:

- **Before the first roll**, and after changing dice, the front face shows the die's name ("d20") in `--die-ink-idle`, semibold, at 0.62× the number size (0.72× for d4, d6 and d8). An unrolled die then can't be read as a result (`renders/sheet-320x568-idle.png`).
- **The result stays** on the die until you roll again, change dice or close the sheet. Remembering the chosen sides in the saved game (for example `diceSides`) is optional. Don't save results.
- **True random**: `crypto.getRandomValues` with rejection sampling, so there is no modulo bias (`rollValue` in `dice.js`). A d10 reads 1–10.
- **During a roll** (720ms), taps on the stage, Roll and the picker are ignored. Fire `haptic()` once, when the die lands.
- Android back and Escape close the sheet like the other dialogs. Include it in `syncBackHandling` and `closeOpenDialog`.

## 3. Die shapes (`shapes.js`)

Each die is drawn on a 100 × 100 viewBox, upright, with light from the upper left. Each entry has an `outline` (the silhouette, clockwise from the top), `faces` (each `{ kind: 'front' | 'side', tone: 0–3, points }`, painted in order) and a `number` (`{ x, y, size, baseline }`, the box centre and font size in viewBox units). The number's font size fits the die's widest label: two digits on d10, d12 and d20. `pointsAttribute()` formats points for `<polygon points>`.

| Die | Silhouette | Front face | Side faces | Number size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| d4 | triangle, side 96, top at y 5 | inset triangle (9-unit bevel) | 3 bevels | 35 |
| d6 | rounded square 82, r 12 | inset rounded square 65, r 6.5 | 4 bevels, split at the corner diagonals | 40 |
| d8 | hexagon, R 48, point up | triangle joining alternate corners | 3 triangles | 40 |
| d10 | diamond (50,2) (97,54) (50,98) (3,54) | kite sharing the top point, other corners at (22,56) (50,77) (78,56) | 4 faces, each kite corner joined to an outline corner | 29 |
| d12 | pentagon, R 50, box centred | inner pentagon R 31 | 5 spokes to the outline corners | 32 |
| d20 | hexagon, R 48 | inset triangle R 34.5 | each triangle corner joined to 3 outline corners: 3 faces beside the front and a ring of 6 | 26 |

Visual weight: the square is drawn smaller (82) and the triangle and diamond use the full box, so the six sit at a similar optical size in a row (`renders/dice-large.png`). Single digits are capped at 40, so a d6 doesn't shout next to a d20.

| `--die-side-1` | `#b8c2ab` | side faces turned toward the light |
| `--die-side-2` | `#97a28b` | side faces at right angles to the light |
| `--die-side-3` | `#78836d` | side faces turned away (3.1:1 against the front face, 4.5:1 against the stage) |
| `--die-edge` | `#566152` | facet edges, 1.5px |
| `--die-outline` | `#2a332b` | silhouette, 2.5px, drawn last |
| `--die-ink` | `#1b2218` | number, 12.6:1 on the front face |
| `--die-ink-idle` | `#5b6656` | "d20" before the first roll, 4.7:1 |
| `--die-shadow` | `#070a08` at opacity .6 | outline copy offset (2%, 4%) under the die |

### Die colours

Each die has its own hue, set by `[data-sides]` on its SVGs in `web/styles.css`. The ramp keeps the sage die's OKLCH lightness steps (front .91, sides .80 / .70 / .59, edge .48, outline .31, ink .24, idle ink .48) with more chroma (.05 on the front, up to .085 on the sides). Hues follow the seat colours: d4 coral (30°), d6 amber (80°), d8 green (135°), d10 teal (185°), d12 blue (245°), d20 violet (305°). On every die the ink is at least 12.5:1 on the front face and the idle "d20" label at least 4.9:1. Unselected picker icons draw in their die's front colour, at least 13:1 on the sheet. The shadow is unchanged.

Strokes use `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` and `stroke-linejoin: round` (set in CSS), so they stay 1.5px and 2.5px whatever the stage size. Give the shadow and body SVGs `position: relative` with z-index 0 and 1. The shadow's opacity makes a stacking context that would otherwise paint over the die.

Number type: `--font-ui`, weight 750 (`--weight-heavy`), `tabular-nums`, tracking −0.04em (the same face as the life totals), `text-anchor="middle"`, `y = number.baseline` (centre + 0.36 × size). It stays upright, so 6 and 9 need no underline. On a 282px stage (a 209px die) the d20's number is set at about 54px, with digits about 39px tall, and the d6's at about 84px. Checked in Chromium and WebKit.

Picker icon: the same geometry in `currentColor`. Edges are 1.25px and the outline 1.75px. The front face is unfilled, and the side faces use `fill-opacity` .14, .30 and .46 for tones 1–3. The icon inverts cleanly on the selected (accent) button and still reads at 16px (`renders/dice-small.png`). Icons show no number.

## 4. Roll animation (WAAPI, transforms and opacity only)

Duration 720ms. The die SVG and its shadow animate separately. `spin` is ±1, chosen at random for each roll. The die lands at 78% (562ms).

Die (`tumbleFrames` in `dice.js`). Each easing applies to the segment that starts at that keyframe.

| Offset | Transform | Easing into the next frame |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `translate(0,0) rotate(0) scale(1)` | `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)` |
| .10 | `translate(0,2%) rotate(−8°·spin) scale(.94)` (wind-up squash) | `cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.4,1)` |
| .42 | `translate(0,−7%) rotate(210°·spin) scale(1.1)` (in the air, nearer) | `cubic-bezier(.5,0,.9,.5)` |
| .78 | `translate(0,0) rotate(366°·spin) scale(.95)` (lands, overshoots 6°) | `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)` |
| .90 | `rotate(358°·spin) scale(1.02)` | `cubic-bezier(.3,0,.3,1)` |
| 1 | `rotate(360°) scale(1)` | |

Shadow (`shadowFrames`): the same rotation keyframes. At rest it sits at `translate(2%,4%)`, opacity .6. In the air (.42) it drifts to `translate(5%,12%) scale(.9)`, opacity .28. At landing (.78) it is at `translate(1.5%,3%) scale(.97)`, opacity .68, then it returns to rest.

Number flicker: the text changes at 0, 45, 95, 150, 210, 275, 350 and 440ms. The gaps grow, so the flicker slows down. Each value is random and never repeats the one before. At 562ms the result appears, `haptic()` fires, and the number pops `scale(1.16 → 1)` over 200ms with `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)` (`transform-box: fill-box; transform-origin: center`). `renders/roll-filmstrip.png` shows the frames.

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no movement and no flicker. The number fades out (90ms, ease-in), the result is set and the haptic fires, then the number fades in (160ms, ease-out). The whole roll takes 250ms.

## 5. Life total at 0 or below

**`--life-out: #f49993`**, which is OKLCH(0.775 0.11 24): a muted coral-red with the same chroma as the accent (0.11). Use it on `.score[data-out]`.

| Panel | p1 | p2 | p3 | p4 | p5 | p6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contrast | 5.00 | 4.84 | 6.14 | 6.27 | **4.79** (lowest) | 5.74 |

Anything less light than L 0.765 drops p5 below 4.5:1, so this is close to the darkest (and so the most red-looking) value that passes on every panel.

It sits 11 ΔE away from player 3's salmon seat glyph `#ffc7b2`, which is also the field-error colour. Next to that glyph it reads as a deeper red, not the same colour.

Deuteranopia (Machado 2009, full severity): it becomes a dull khaki, `#c2b892`, about 18 ΔE darker and duller than the cream `--text`. A deutan player sees the total dim and turn tan rather than red. It is a clear change, but not "red". The value itself (0 or a real minus sign) remains the main cue, which is fine because the colour only repeats what the number already says.

`renders/life-out.png` and `renders/board-4p-844x390.png` show it on all six panels.

## Not in scope

Several dice at once, a roll history, and a sheet that turns to face the seat that opened it. The entry point is shared, so the app can't know which seat that is.
