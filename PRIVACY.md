# LifePod privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

LifePod is a life counter for tabletop games. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.

## What LifePod stores

LifePod saves the current game on your device so it is still there the next time you open the app:

- the player count, starting life, and each player's life total
- each seat's rotation and tile colour
- each player's commander damage, poison, commander tax, energy, experience, rad and colour identity
- what tiles show of each colour identity
- the mana floating in each player's pool
- the turn: who went first, whose turn it is, and the round
- who is the monarch and who has the initiative
- the last 50 dice rolls and coin flips, with when each landed, and the die last picked
- whether **Keep awake**, the **Commander**, **Poison**, **Energy**, **Experience** and **Rad** counters, **Monarch**, **Initiative**, **Mana pool** and emptying mana pools when the turn passes are on, the **Turns** setting, and the **Reminders** switches

This stays on your device: in the app's private storage on Android and iOS, or in your browser's local storage on the web. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

If you use Android's device backup or iCloud backup, your phone may include LifePod's saved game in that backup. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under your account settings. LifePod's developer never receives them.

## What LifePod does not do

- The Android app has no internet permission, and neither the Android nor the iOS app makes network requests.
- There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
- LifePod does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

**Keep awake** only asks the operating system to keep the screen on while LifePod is open.

## Children

LifePod is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
